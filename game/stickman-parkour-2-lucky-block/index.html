Stickman Parkour 2: Lucky Block Unblocked

by Artur Stogney

Sprint across floating block worlds in Stickman Parkour 2: Lucky Block! Crack open mystery lucky blocks, dodge perilous traps, and reach the portal in record time.

Stickman Parkour 2: Lucky Block is an exhilarating 2D platform runner set in a vibrant cubic universe inspired by Minecraft. Guide a nimble stickman as he dashes over floating islands, leaps across gaping ravines, and scales sheer cliff faces to reach the safety of the end portal.

Smash open mysterious Lucky Blocks along your route to trigger unpredictable outcomes—from handy speed potions and wings to sudden TNT blasts and enemy ambushes. Master fluid wall-jumps, slide under low barriers, and race against the clock to conquer dozens of handcrafted parkour levels.

How to play Stickman Parkour 2: Lucky Block

- Desktop: Use A / D or Left / Right Arrow keys to run, and W or Up Arrow / Spacebar to jump and perform wall-jumps. - Mobile: Tap the left and right directional arrows to run, and tap the jump icon to leap over platforms.

About this game

Controls WASD or Arrow keys to run and jump; W/Up to wall-jump.
